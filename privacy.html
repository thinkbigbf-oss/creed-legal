Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Last updated / 最后更新：2026-09-15

Creed to Habit ("we", "the app") helps you define personal creeds (principles), track the behaviors that put them into practice, and reflect on your progress. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices. 本应用帮助你定义个人信条、记录践行信条的行为并复盘进展。本政策说明我们收集哪些数据、如何使用，以及你的选择权。

1. Information We Collect / 我们收集的信息

We do not collect your data. Creed to Habit has no account, no sign-in and no server. Everything you enter — creeds, explanations, sources, categories, behaviors, check-ins, events, version history and reminder settings — is stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us or to any third party. 我们不收集你的数据。本应用没有账号、无需登录，也没有服务器。你录入的信条、释义、出处、分类、行为、打卡、事件、版本历史与提醒设置，只保存在你自己的设备上，不会上传给我们或任何第三方。

2. How the App Works / 应用如何运行

The app works fully offline. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Share posters are generated on your device, and are only sent where you choose to share them. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. 应用完全离线运行。提醒是在你设备上安排的本地通知；分享海报在设备上生成，只会发送到你自己选择的分享渠道。我们不使用任何统计分析、广告或追踪。

3. Backup / 备份

Your data is included in your device's own backups (for example iCloud or computer backups), which are controlled by you and Apple under your device settings. If you delete the app, the data on that device is deleted with it. 你的数据会随设备自身的备份（如 iCloud 备份或电脑备份）一起备份，这些备份由你在系统设置中自行控制。删除本应用即会删除该设备上的数据。

4. Accounts from Earlier Versions / 早期版本的账号

Earlier versions of the app offered an optional account with cloud sync. If you used one, the first time you open the current version on a signed-in device, your records are downloaded once to that device, and the app stops connecting to the cloud afterwards. To permanently delete a legacy account and any data still stored in the cloud, email dcjwf@qq.com from the email address of that account, and we will delete it. 本应用的早期版本曾提供可选账号与云同步。如果你用过，在已登录的设备上首次打开当前版本时，你的记录会被一次性下载到该设备，此后应用不再连接云端。如需永久删除早期版本的账号及仍存于云端的数据，请用该账号的邮箱发邮件至上述地址，我们会为你删除。

5. Security / 安全

Because your data stays on your device, it is protected by your device's own security (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID and encryption). 由于数据只保存在你的设备上，它受你设备自身的安全机制（锁屏密码、面容/触控 ID 与加密）保护。

6. Children / 儿童

Creed to Habit is not directed to children under 13. 本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童。

7. Changes / 政策变更

We may update this policy and will revise the date above. 我们可能更新本政策并修订上方日期。
